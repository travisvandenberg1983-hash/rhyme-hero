Privacy Policy

Rhyme Hero · Effective 18 October 2026

Rhyme Hero is a rhyming skill coach. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Words you look up. When you ask for a word bank, the word you typed is sent to the Rhyme Hero engine so it can find rhymes. The engine does not store it.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting providers (Vercel and Render) keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and time of request, to run and secure the service.
  • Fonts. The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your information for marketing.
  • The app does not access your microphone, contacts or location.

Future features

If we add accounts, saved progress or voice input, we will update this policy before those features go live and explain what is stored, for how long, and how to delete it. Voice recordings will not be kept after transcription.

Children

Rhyme Hero is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
